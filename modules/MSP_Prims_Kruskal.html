Algorithm Explanation

Prim's Algorithm

Prim's algorithm starts with one vertex and repeatedly chooses the minimum-weight edge that connects a vertex already in the MST to a vertex outside the MST. 

Start
  ↓
Choose minimum edge
  ↓
Add new vertex
  ↓
Choose next minimum edge
  ↓
Repeat until all vertices are included

The important idea is that Prim's algorithm grows one connected tree.
